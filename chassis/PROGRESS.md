## v3 (started 2026-10-17) - working assumptions (team: correct any of these and I re-run)
- **Tank**: stands as in v2 (40x40x80), cap + 2 x 90-deg elbow barbs on the TOP 40x40 face, 17 mm tall incl. the tube leaving sideways. No room above a v2 tank (3 mm), so the shell cap gets a raised, rounded **tank dome** (hatch) over the tank only; the rest of the top stays 151.5. Tank and battery drop 2.9 mm onto ribs at Z 63.1 (still 0.5 over the front gearboxes).
- **Pumps**: R385 barbs both on one end, axial (as v1/v2). Lower pump = SPRAY (tank -> nozzle), upper pump = REFILL (arm -> tank).
- **Battery lead**: XT60 (15.5 x 8 x 16 housing) on 14 AWG; balance lead JST-XH 4P stays inside (charging is via the DC port + the pack's BMS).
- **Charge port**: DC-099 metal 5.5x2.1 panel jack (5 A, Ø11 hole) - buy this; charger 12.6 V CC/CV 2 A with a 5.5x**2.1** centre-positive plug. Pack must have a 3S BMS.
- **Main switch**: 20 mm round 20 A @ 12 V snap-in rocker. The E-stop is wired in series with it in the main battery line (pressing it kills everything, the Pi included). If you want the E-stop to cut motors/pumps only, it's a wiring change, not a CAD change.
- **Nozzle**: Luer system - female Luer to 1/4" (6 mm) barb adapter + 14 ga blunt Luer-lock tip (straight stream, no mist). Holder grips the adapter hub (Ø8 assumed, measure yours).
- **Refill arm**: MG90S lying on its side, shaft through the right wall, pivot at Z 70 just ahead of the wheel; arm 80 x 15 x 15, stowed pointing UP flat on the right wall, deployed ~61 deg down-forward so its tip touches the ground ahead of the front wheel. Strainer clipped to the wall when stowed.
- **RGB bar**: Yahboom 14-LED flexible strip, 97 x 12 mm, wrapped round the front and both sides of the LiDAR riser (visible from front + sides, highest point after the LiDAR).
- **AS5600**: 23 x 23 board (holes 15.5-17.5 slotted, chip at centre), 6 x 2.5 diametric magnet in a shell lug on the hinge axis in the free centre span (Y 15-27, the pin doesn't run there), 1-1.5 mm air gap (datasheet 0.5-3).
- **LiDAR beam**: vertical divergence is not published (LD19 / STL-19P datasheets); I assume +-5 mm of beam height near the robot on top of the 10 mm margin. Holes are Ø2.5 -> M2 screws.
- **Wheel gap 4 mm**: set with a printed 8 mm spacer on each shaft between the wall and the coupling (coupling then grips the last 7 mm of the 15 mm shaft beyond the boss, on the D-flat).
